Architecture & trust

Built different from a SaaS.

SmartMido isn’t one shared cloud app that everyone logs into. Every company runs on its own — which is exactly what makes it secure, resilient and yours.

🛡

Isolated, never shared

Unlike a typical SaaS, each company gets its own container and its own database. Your data is physically separated — it can never touch another company’s.

🔑

Permissions you control

Role-based access down to the individual action — every person sees and does exactly what you grant them, and nothing more.

🔄

Nothing is ever lost

A feed can be late or drop entirely; the parsers keep running and backfill the moment data returns — so no ticket slips through.

☁

Work from anywhere

A cloud web app with nothing to install — sign in from a phone, tablet or laptop. All it needs is an internet connection.
